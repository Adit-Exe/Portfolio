Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

SpendBook keeps expense management fast, clear, and effortless — without unnecessary complexity.

Smart Categories

Choose from built-in categories like Travel, Food, Subscription, Electricity, and more — or create your own on the fly.

Auto-Combine Entries

Multiple payments in the same category on the same day are automatically merged into a single record. No duplicates, ever.

Visual Analytics

Interactive pie charts, line graphs, and bar charts help you understand spending trends at a glance — daily, weekly, or monthly.

Automatic Date Tracking

Every payment is automatically tagged with the current date — no manual date selection needed.

Excel Import & Export

Back up your data to .xlsx files or restore from a previous export. Data validation ensures file integrity during import.

Adaptive Theming

SpendBook follows your device's primary color to create a personalized look with dynamic chart shading.

How It Works

Three simple steps.

SpendBook is designed around a two-screen experience — fast to enter, easy to understand.

1

Enter a Payment

Select or type a category, enter the amount, and you're done. The date is handled automatically.

2

View Your Analytics

Swipe to the analytics screen to see interactive charts, category totals, percentages, and trends.

3

Export & Backup

Export your data to Excel anytime. Import it back on a new device with full validation.

About SpendBook

Built around one goal.

Making expense tracking simple enough to use every day while providing enough visual information to understand where your money is going. SpendBook stores records locally with SQLite, keeps navigation gesture-based, and adapts its look to match your device.
